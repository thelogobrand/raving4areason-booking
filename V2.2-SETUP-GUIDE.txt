RAVING 4 A REASON — VERSION 2.2

FINAL PARENT ACCESS DESIGN
- Shared username is pre-filled as R4ARparent.
- Shared login provides access to Book a Session, Available Sessions, Newsletter and Contact.
- Contact appears above the small Make a Donation button.
- Parents do not browse booking history through the shared login.
- Every booking receives a unique booking reference and secure Manage Booking / Messages link by email.
- The secure link shows only that booking and its conversation.
